# C3 AI Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE)

## 1. Nomenclature and Purpose
At C3 AI, the customer-embedded technical organization is called the **Forward Deployed Engineering** team, consisting of **Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs)** and **Forward Deployed Data Scientists (FDDSs)** [1][2].

### Core Purpose
C3 AI builds industrial-scale enterprise AI applications (e.g., predictive maintenance, supply chain optimization, financial fraud detection) [3][4]. The FDE team’s purpose is to embed with customer organizations to act as proactive problem solvers who configure, customize, and scale the C3 AI Platform within the customer's live IT and cloud infrastructure [2][5].
* **Bridging Enterprise Legacy and AI:** Enterprise data resides in highly fragmented legacy environments (historians, relational databases, cloud silos). FDEs design the ingestion pipelines and unified schemas required to feed AI models [1][2].
* **Accelerating AI Application Delivery:** FDEs move AI projects from "conceptual proofs" to "production deployment" rapidly by building customized frontend interfaces and application logic on top of the C3 AI Platform [1][4].
* **Enabling Customer Autonomy:** FDEs mentor and train the customer’s internal teams, ensuring they can manage and scale the AI applications themselves after the engagement [4][2].

## 2. Team Structure & Roles
C3 AI deploys cross-functional, highly coordinated customer-embedded units consisting of the following key profiles [1][2]:

### A. Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)
* **Objective:** Lead the technical implementation, system integration, and custom software development [2][3].
* **Responsibilities:**
  * Write clean, production-grade custom application logic and UI components [1][4].
  * Build and optimize complex data ingestion and processing pipelines [4][3].
  * Handle database tuning, system performance optimization, and deep debugging [4][3].
  * Integrate C3 AI applications into the customer’s legacy software architectures [2].
* **Talent Profile:** Elite software engineers with deep expertise in systems architecture, databases, cloud platforms, and data structures [1][4]. They must possess consulting polish and be highly professional in high-pressure, client-facing settings [4][3].

### B. Forward Deployed Data Scientist (FDDS)
* **Objective:** Design, train, and deploy enterprise-grade machine learning models tailored to the client's business problems [1][5].
* **Responsibilities:**
  * Map ambiguous business problems to specific ML objectives (e.g., predicting equipment failure, optimizing inventory levels) [1][4].
  * Clean, validate, and explore historical client datasets [1].
  * Train, evaluate, and tune ML algorithms using the C3 AI Studio and AutoML platforms [4][6][5].
  * Translate complex mathematical model behaviors to non-technical client stakeholders [1][7].
* **Talent Profile:** Highly analytical mathematical minds (typically holding STEM degrees) with practical software engineering skills and a strong grasp of applied ML, statistics, and domain economics [1][4].

### C. AI Solution Manager
* **Objective:** Act as the strategic lead, defining the roadmap, scope, and high-level value metrics of the AI deployment [1].
* **Responsibilities:**
  * Define and measure key economic value drivers for the AI project [1].
  * Coordinate activities, track deliverables, manage timelines, and serve as the main business-technical interface [1].
  * Lead post-sales customer adaptation, platform integration strategy, and change management [1].
* **Talent Profile:** Elite, tier-1 hybrid talent. The expectations for C3 AI's AI Solution Managers are exceptionally high compared to traditional project or product management roles. They must combine strong technical know-how (frequently having a hands-on technical or engineering background), rigorous product management capability, and executive consulting polish. Because of this rare and highly demanding combination, these professionals are highly sought after; they often proceed to found their own technology startups or are poached aggressively by fast-moving AI startups that have adapted rapidly to deployment-driven market needs.

## 3. Talent Characteristics and Profile
C3 AI hires for a distinct "builder-consultant" profile, placing a premium on hybrid capability [1][4][2]:
* **Strong Engineering Fundamentals:** Unlike pure IT consultants, C3 AI FDEs are expected to write production code and understand algorithms, system scalability, and cloud architecture [1][4].
* **Domain Expertise:** Candidates with deep domain knowledge in target sectors (e.g., Oil & Gas, Utilities, Defense, Manufacturing, Aerospace) are highly valued as they can immediately translate operational friction into AI logic [1].
* **Comfort with Ambiguity:** FDEs must be able to decompose vague customer requests into concrete, structured technical specifications [1][6].
