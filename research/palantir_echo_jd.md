# Job Description: Palantir - Deployment Strategist (Echo)

## Role Overview
At Palantir, Deployment Strategists ("Echos") are tier-1 elite hybrid professionals who act as the primary strategic integration layer between Palantir's platform capabilities (Foundry, Gotham, AIP) and our clients' operational missions. This role combines product management, technical strategy, data analysis, and high-level business consulting. Echos work directly with client executives and end-user analysts to design user-centric workflows, oversee implementation, and ensure that our software drives immediate, measurable ROI.

---

## Core Responsibilities
* **Operational Immersive Discovery:** Embed with customer operations teams to deeply understand their workflows, map out data silos, and identify the highest-impact business or mission challenges.
* **Workflow & Solution Design:** Collaborate with client users and Palantir Forward Deployed Engineers (Deltas) to design, configure, and deploy custom applications and user-centric data ontologies.
* **Technical Scoping & Data Partnership:** Partner with Deltas to identify mission-critical datasets, design schema mappings, and ensure technical efforts align with operational requirements.
* **Project & Stakeholder Management:** Own client relationship management, set operational milestones, manage timelines, and present technical outcomes to stakeholders ranging from frontline operators to C-suite executives.
* **User Enablement & Adoption:** Lead hands-on training sessions and hackathons to drive rapid software adoption and self-sufficiency across the client's organization.
* **Product Feedback Loop:** Synthesize field observations, user friction points, and feature requests to guide internal product engineering roadmaps.

---

## Required Qualifications
* **Experience & Education:** Bachelor's or Master's degree in a highly analytical field (Business, Economics, Engineering, Mathematics, etc.) with a track record of high academic or professional achievement.
* **Professional Competency:** 
  * Exceptional problem-solving capabilities with a structured, analytical approach to open-ended, complex issues.
  * Elite communication, presentation, and interpersonal skills; ability to command respect in high-stakes boardroom or operational settings.
* **Technical Literacy:** Demonstrated familiarity with technical concepts. While not a strict coding role, experience or proficiency with scripting and data analysis tools (**SQL, Python, R, or Matlab**) is highly preferred.
* **Mindset & Cultural Fit:**
  * High agency, low ego, and exceptional user empathy.
  * Exceptional adaptability, resilience, and comfort operating in chaotic, unstructured, or fast-paced environments.
* **Logistics:** High willingness to travel (typically 25% to 75%, depending on customer engagement).

---

## Preferred Qualifications
* Prior experience at a top-tier management consulting firm (MBB), product management at a high-growth tech firm, or specialized industry domain expertise.
* Active US Government Security Clearance (Secret, Top Secret, or higher) for government-facing deployments.
