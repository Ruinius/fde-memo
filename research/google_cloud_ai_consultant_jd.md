# Job Description: Google Cloud - AI Consultant (Professional Services Organization)

## Role Overview
Within Google Cloud's Professional Services Organization (PSO), the AI Consultant is a non-technical, delivery-focused role. Positioned to guide enterprise customers through the conceptual and organizational phases of their Generative AI journeys, this role focuses heavily on project management, administrative coordination, and strategic business alignment.

Unlike Google Cloud's Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) or Solutions Engineers (SEs) who write code and design systems, AI Consultants operate primarily as administrative facilitators. In practice, due to Google's massive organizational size and deeply entrenched, engineering-dominated culture, this role functions predominantly as a standard Project Manager. Without hands-on keyboard technical execution or deep systems authority, AI Consultants operate as administrative coordinators who manage project schedules, coordinate meetings, track task completions, and write corporate alignment presentations.

---

## Core Responsibilities
* **Project & Delivery Management:** Track project timelines, coordinate weekly status syncs, manage administrative deliverables, and maintain standard progress tracking sheets for GenAI rollouts.
* **Meeting & Workshop Facilitation:** Organize and schedule design thinking workshops, coordinate meetings between customer business leaders and Google engineering teams, and document key takeaways.
* **Business Use-Case Alignment:** Help client business leaders catalog potential AI use cases, map them to high-level corporate goals, and prepare slide decks outlining deployment roadmaps.
* **Cross-Functional Administrative Support:** Act as the administrative liaison between Google's internal sales teams, embedded engineers (FDEs), and customer project management offices (PMOs).
* **Change Management Coordination:** Coordinate basic organizational change activities, distribute training materials, and schedule enablement sessions for client end-users.
* **Status Reporting & Presentation Development:** Draft executive status reports and presentation slides to keep Google and client leadership updated on implementation milestones.

---

## Required Qualifications
* **Educational Background:** Bachelor's degree in Business, Communications, Management, Information Systems, or a related field. PMP (Project Management Professional) or Agile Scrum certification is highly valued.
* **Professional Experience:**
  * 3+ years of experience in project management, business consulting, or administrative coordination within a tech environment.
  * Experience managing client-facing schedules, tracking complex workflows, and preparing corporate presentations.
* **Skills & Competencies:**
  * Strong organization and administrative scheduling skills.
  * Proficiency in building slide presentations, maintaining project trackers, and writing status briefs.
  * Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
* **Logistics:** Willingness to travel as required by the client engagement (typically 20% to 50%).

---

## Preferred Qualifications
* Prior experience as a standard project manager (PM) or scrum master in an IT consulting firm or large enterprise.
* Familiarity with general AI/ML terminology and cloud service models (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS) at a conceptual, non-technical level.
* Experience navigating administrative processes and project lifecycles within very large, matrixed corporate environments.
