# Job Description: Google Cloud - Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE / Applied AI & PSO)

## Role Overview
At Google Cloud, the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) within the Applied AI and Professional Services Organization (PSO) is an embedded software and systems builder who bridges the critical gap between high-level architectural proposals and live, enterprise-grade AI production. 

While traditional Google Solutions Engineers (SEs) focus on pre-sales advisory and mock-data proof-of-concepts to secure platform wins, Google Cloud FDEs are late-stage GTM builders who own high-consequence implementations. Embedded directly inside the actual network perimeters and secure cloud environments of strategic enterprise accounts, FDEs write production-grade, low-latency, and highly secure code using Python, Vertex AI, Vertex Search, and BigQuery. FDEs build the complex data integration loops, vector indexes, and prompt orchestration layers needed to operationalize frontier models (like Gemini) and Vertex AI platforms while returning field insights to central R&D to improve Google Cloud's product portfolio.

---

## Core Responsibilities
* **Production-Grade MLOps & Building:** Write, optimize, and deploy highly reliable and secure production code to operationalize Gemini models and the Vertex AI platform inside the customer's live cloud infrastructure.
* **Complex Data Integration & Plumbing:** Design and build large-scale data ingestion pipelines, vector databases (Vertex Vector Search), BigQuery integrations, and prompt orchestration layers to feed live business systems.
* **Embedded Client Collaboration:** Embed directly with client software engineering, database administration, and security teams (on-site) to navigate complex security review boards, legacy database environments, and custom cloud perimeters.
* **System Portability & Open Architecture:** Architect portable, open-standards systems (leveraging containers and open-source frameworks) to ease complex hybrid-cloud and multi-cloud integrations.
* **Late-Stage Implementation Ownership:** Own the technical implementation lifecycle from late-stage GTM through deployment, testing, performance tuning, and launch, securing measurable operational ROI.
* **Vertex AI Product Feedback Loop:** Document deployment bottlenecks, API limitations, and architectural friction in the field, collaborating directly with central Vertex AI R&D and product engineering teams to generalize these findings into core product features.

---

## Required Qualifications
* **Educational Background:** Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a highly technical STEM field.
* **Professional Experience:**
  * 3+ years of professional software engineering, systems engineering, or MLOps experience.
  * Hands-on experience building and deploying generative AI systems, RAG architectures, or complex data processing pipelines in enterprise environments.
* **Technical Skills:**
  * High proficiency in **Python** and **SQL**.
  * Deep experience with the Google Cloud Platform (GCP) ecosystem, specifically **BigQuery, Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE), Vertex AI, and Cloud Run**.
  * Solid understanding of data structures, containerization (Docker, Kubernetes), and systems integration.
* **Mindset & Soft Skills:**
  * High professional agency, resilience under pressure, and a proactive problem-solving mindset.
  * Strong communication and stakeholder management skills; ability to collaborate with enterprise CTOs and navigate strict corporate security committees.
* **Logistics:** High willingness to travel to client sites as required (typically 25% to 75%).

---

## Preferred Qualifications
* Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect or Professional Data Engineer certification.
* Experience deploying generative AI models, vector databases, and MLOps/LLMOps pipelines.
* Deep knowledge of corporate network security (VPCs, firewalls, IAM, private endpoints) and enterprise compliance frameworks (SOC2, HIPAA, GDPR).
* Prior experience in management consulting, systems integration, or client-facing technical delivery.
