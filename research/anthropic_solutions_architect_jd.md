# Job Description: Anthropic - Solutions Architect (SA)

## Role Overview
At Anthropic, the Solutions Architect (SA) is a highly strategic, technical, and client-facing professional who drives pre-sales architectural guidance, GTM partner enablement, and ecosystem-wide technical education. SAs sit at the nexus of GTM strategy, cloud platform partnerships, and enterprise engineering, ensuring customers and partners can build highly scalable, efficient, and robust systems around Claude.

SAs focus on system-wide breadth, GTM strategy, and relationship management. Unlike Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) who embed deeply with a small number of clients to write production code, SAs scale Anthropic's technical capabilities across the broader ecosystem. You will partner with major cloud hyperscalers (e.g., AWS Bedrock, Google Cloud Vertex AI) and Global System Integrators (GSIs like Accenture, Deloitte) to develop reference architectures, lead developer workshops, and enable partner engineers to build scalable business practices around Claude.

---

## Core Responsibilities
* **Ecosystem & GTM Enablement:** Build and scale deep technical partnerships with key cloud providers (AWS Bedrock, GCP Vertex AI) and GSIs (Accenture, Deloitte, BCG) to expand Claude's market reach.
* **Reference Architecture Design:** Develop, publish, and maintain state-of-the-art reference architectures, white papers, and open-source code templates to guide developers on best-practice integrations.
* **Pre-Sales Architectural Guidance:** Partner with account executives during the pre-sales discovery phase to design secure, scalable, and high-performance system architectures for strategic enterprise prospects.
* **Developer Workshops & Training:** Design and deliver technical workshops, hackathons, and educational bootcamps to train partner engineers and enterprise developers on advanced Claude APIs, model parameters, and RAG architectures.
* **System Integration Scoping:** Assess client system landscapes, data flows, and security requirements to recommend optimal deployment methods (SaaS, VPC, or partner cloud).
* **Broad Market Feedback Loop:** Gather broad developer feedback, API limitations, and general market requirements to influence central product engineering and developer relations roadmaps.

---

## Required Qualifications
* **Educational Background:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, or a related analytical STEM field.
* **Professional Experience:**
  * 5+ years of experience in solutions architecture, technical consulting, developer relations, or pre-sales engineering in cloud computing or enterprise SaaS.
  * Practical experience designing and architecting applications with generative AI, vector databases, or complex API integrations.
* **Technical Skills:**
  * Broad understanding of enterprise cloud architectures (specifically AWS and GCP ecosystem tools).
  * High conceptual and practical literacy in LLM prompt design, API integration, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and data flow management.
  * Proficiency in a programming language (preferably **Python** or **JavaScript**) for building demonstrations, templates, and script examples.
* **Soft Skills & Relationship Management:**
  * Exceptional presentation, teaching, and writing skills; ability to articulate complex architectural patterns clearly to both junior developers and C-suite executives.
  * Highly strategic mindset with strong business acumen and relationship management skills.
* **Logistics:** Willingness to travel (typically 10% to 30% depending on partner and customer engagement).

---

## Preferred Qualifications
* AWS Certified Solutions Architect or Google Cloud Professional Cloud Architect certification.
* Prior experience working at a major cloud hyperscaler (AWS, GCP, Azure) or a tier-1 Global System Integrator (GSI).
* Deep understanding of enterprise security, SSO, RBAC, and data privacy regulations (HIPAA, GDPR, SOC2).
