# Job Description: C3 AI - Forward Deployed Data Scientist (FDDS)

## Role Overview
The Forward Deployed Data Scientist (FDDS) at C3 AI is a hybrid technical-consulting role. Embedded directly with customer teams, the FDDS is responsible for translating ambiguous business problems (such as predicting asset downtime or optimizing supply chains) into concrete mathematical objectives, designing, training, and deploying enterprise-grade machine learning models on the C3 AI Platform. Unlike traditional data science roles, this position demands hands-on field implementation, managing raw, messy client datasets, and translating technical model behaviors for non-technical stakeholders.

---

## Core Responsibilities
* **Customer-Embedded Model Design:** Design, develop, and deploy scalable ML models that solve specific, mission-critical business problems within client environments.
* **Full-Lifecycle Modeling:** Lead the data science lifecycle on site: conduct data exploration, cleaning, validation, feature engineering, and model training/evaluation.
* **Platform Implementation:** Utilize the C3 AI Platform (including C3 AI Studio and AutoML) to implement, train, and test models on high-throughput enterprise data pipelines.
* **Technical Communication & Translation:** Act as a technical advisor to customer teams, translating complex ML methodologies, data limitations, and model evaluations into clear, actionable business strategies.
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Partner with C3 AI Forward Deployed Engineers, Product Managers, and customer subject matter experts (SMEs) to ensure models are production-ready and integrated.
* **MLOps & Observability:** Configure automated model evaluation, monitoring, and retraining pipelines inside the customer's production cloud infrastructure.

---

## Required Qualifications
* **Educational Background:** Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD in an analytical STEM field (Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Operations Research, Physics, or related).
* **Technical Skills:**
  * Strong command of data science fundamentals, machine learning algorithms, and statistical analysis.
  * Proficiency in coding, specifically **Python** and **SQL**, along with experience building data processing pipelines.
  * Experience with popular ML libraries (TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, Pandas).
* **Consulting & Soft Skills:**
  * Exceptional verbal and written communication skills with the ability to explain complex statistical models to non-technical stakeholders.
  * High organization and ability to operate independently in high-stakes customer-facing settings.
* **Logistics:** Willingness to travel to client sites as required by the engagement.

---

## Preferred Qualifications
* Prior experience productionizing machine learning models (MLOps) inside cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP).
* Familiarity with specific enterprise platform architectures (e.g., C3 AI Studio).
* Experience handling high-velocity time-series or IoT sensor data.
